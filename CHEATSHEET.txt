# Cheatsheet

ToLaserBlade-ExtraModels
version 0.2.0

- [ToLaserBlade-ExtraModels](https://github.com/Iunius118/ToLaserBlade-ExtraModels)

This mod is a ToLaserBlade add-on to add more laser blade shapes.

## Requirements

- NeoForge/Fabric/Forge
  - ToLaserBlade v17.0.0+
  - https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tolaserblade
  - https://modrinth.com/mod/tolaserblade
